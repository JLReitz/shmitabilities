# Work through questions

## 1. Load the question

Read the ticket and the decision that needs its answer. Load other related tickets only when their detail is needed.

## 2. Research the answer

Invoke the `shmitty-research` skill. Use primary documentation, source code, specifications, first-party APIs, or local knowledge bases.

## 3. Record the findings

Write a source-backed answer in the ticket. Link to research artifacts and state the facts that affect the dependent decision.
